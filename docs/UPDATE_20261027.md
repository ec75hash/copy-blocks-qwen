## Scientific boundaries retained

- Qwen2.5 has a verified width increase, not a measured channel-by-channel MLP
  recipe. Endpoint residuals are not assumed dated training updates; cosine
  does not calibrate elapsed tokens or duplication time.

- Within Qwen3-32B, layers 32–55 repeat layers 8–31. Between checkpoints,
  14B layers 0–9 match 32B layers 0–9 best; 14B layers 10–39 match 32B
  layers 34–63 best. These are two different comparisons.
- First-17,408 gate-row correspondence is neither exact equality nor a nearest-neighbor
  proof for every neuron. Higher-index-row searches are sampled at five layers and include
  early-layer exceptions. The group-aware head map retains six exceptions out of 896.
- Shared construction/growth is an interpretation, not a demonstrated training history
  or a claim that the released 14B was necessarily the direct parent of the released 32B.
- The Q8 response study is descriptive; its partner-ranking result accompanies small
  median correlation and low median strongest-passage overlap.

## Reproduction and validation

See [REPRODUCTION_V020.md](REPRODUCTION_V020.md). The package includes two kinds of
checks: hashes/inventory and independent reductions of the retained numerical evidence.
The supplied checks recompute retained measurements; no second full inference collection or full tensor scan is included.
The retained independent weight check records which raw payloads were checked and unavailable.
